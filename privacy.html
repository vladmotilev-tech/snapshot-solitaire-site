Snapshot Solitaire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Snapshot Solitaire (the “Game”) is developed by AI pirates Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and how.

Information we collect

The current version of the Game does not collect, store on our servers or share any personal information. It does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and does not use the Android advertising ID.

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

Advertising

We plan to show ads in future versions of the Game. Ad networks may then collect device information (such as the advertising ID, device model, IP address and app usage) to show and measure ads. Before that happens we will update this policy, list each ad partner and its privacy policy, and, where the law requires it (for example in the EU/UK), ask for your consent in the Game.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game’s release notes.
